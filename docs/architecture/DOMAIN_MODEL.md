# XTEN canonical domain model

**Status:** PROPOSED · **Date:** 2026-09-24 · **Scope:** M01-T01. This conceptual language supports the accepted [product contract](../PRODUCT.md) and [system architecture](SYSTEM_ARCHITECTURE.md); it is not a database or ORM schema.

## Identity, ownership and scope rules

A Tenant is the isolation and customer-data ownership boundary. An Engagement is one consultancy service contract and delivery scope within a Tenant; a tenant may have multiple engagements with distinct entitlements, owners and periods. A CloudConnection is the authorised credential/trust relationship to one provider environment for a Tenant. A CloudScope is the explicit account/subscription/project, data type, permission and time scope permitted under that connection. Neither an engagement nor a connection substitutes for tenant membership. An engagement does not grant cloud access, and a connection does not by itself authorise every source or action.

Tenant-owned entities carry an explicit immutable tenant_id in their identity and persistence context; cross-tenant reassignment is forbidden. External-facing IDs are opaque standard non-sequential identifiers (for example UUID/ULID-class); no custom cryptographic algorithm or sequential customer count is exposed. Human User identity and generic role vocabulary are system/global records, but Membership, rights, CloudConnection, data, workflow, reports and audit events are tenant-scoped. A Tenant registry record identifies its own tenant_id; global administration must not become unscoped access to tenant payloads. Provider-native identifiers are recorded as attributes and never replace XTEN tenant-scoped IDs.

All money-bearing domain values carry exact amount, currency, cost basis/type, period/context, and source/calculation version where relevant. A missing value is unknown, not zero. Derived totals cannot silently combine currencies, bases or periods. Record and publication version references are immutable once used by a published snapshot.

## Cross-aggregate rules

1. The tenancy module owns tenant identity and membership context. A User can belong to multiple Tenants, but each command selects exactly one authorised tenant. Cross-tenant reporting or support access requires a separately designed, explicitly authorised path.
2. An Engagement scopes service obligations; CloudConnection scopes trust; CloudScope scopes permitted provider targets and data. These four concepts have separate IDs and independent life cycles.
3. Collector credentials are read-only and bound to CloudConnection/CloudScope. They cannot create or inherit an Approval, ImplementationRecord execution right or future execution-plane credential.
4. SourceArtifact and SourceRecordIdentity are immutable evidence identities. CanonicalCostDataset, allocation and calculation changes create versioned derivatives; provider corrections do not alter already-published evidence.
5. Recommendation, Approval, ImplementationRecord and Measurement are separate aggregates. Approval is a human decision on a revision, implementation is a recorded change, and measured benefit requires a versioned agreed Baseline and observed result.
6. Money values and outputs preserve currency, cost basis, period, source and calculation/policy version. Exact decimal arithmetic is authoritative; negative credits are valid, NULL/unknown differs from zero, and cross-currency sums require versioned FX policy.
7. AllocationPolicy and AllocationVersion preserve history. Unknown owner remains unknown, while any allocated total reconciles to the source amount including visible unallocated/residual amounts.
8. ReportSnapshot is immutable at publication and binds all contributing versions, template, publisher and time. Export is a distinct distribution artifact with current access controls. Audit history records decisions but does not authorise actions.
9. Jobs and outbox records (operational/control-plane concepts, not full business aggregates) carry tenant, operation, version and idempotency identity. A retry rechecks current authority and cannot silently substitute new source or policy versions.

## System/global records and later physical design

The system/global catalog contains identity-provider issuer configuration, User identity subject mappings, role vocabulary/policy definitions, supported provider/adapter/schema versions, application-level feature/support matrix versions and system administration audit events. A tenant-specific grant, connection, provider source, recommendation, report or audit event remains tenant-owned even when it references a global definition. Global definitions confer no tenant access by themselves.

M01-T02 must settle financial cost bases, provider mapping and versioned support entries; M01-T03 must settle data classification, retention and abuse cases; M01-T04 must define measurable acceptance targets. M02 may then design physical schemas and precise transaction/RLS policy tests under accepted ADRs. No entity list here asserts that a table, cloud resource or application exists.
